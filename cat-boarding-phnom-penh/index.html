HOME CAT BOARDING • PHNOM PENH

Cat boarding in Phnom Penh, with a gentle approach

Cats often need a little more time to settle into a new space. At Pet House, cats are cared for in a home environment in Phnom Penh, with attention to familiar habits, rest and comfort. Our home is air-conditioned, and we take only a limited number of animal guests at once. This is home-based cat boarding rather than a cage-based pet hotel.

How we care for cats

A calm home environment

We give each cat time to settle in at its own pace. Tell us whether your cat is sociable, shy, playful or prefers quiet time.

Familiar meal and care routines

Share your cat's feeding schedule, preferred food, litter preferences and any special instructions so we can plan their care.

Photo and video updates

Regular updates help owners see how their cats are doing during the stay.

Care tailored to the animal

We can discuss needs for kittens, senior cats and pets that need additional attention. Medication can be considered when clear, safe instructions are available.

What to tell us before booking

Please share your cat's age, temperament, vaccination and parasite-protection status, food routine, any medical needs and the dates of your trip. Tell us whether your cat has lived around other animals. We carefully review whether a stay is suitable alongside pets already in our care, and can arrange a visit before the booking.

Arranging the stay

Contact us with your dates to confirm availability and the rate for your cat's stay. Pickup and drop-off around Phnom Penh may be possible depending on dates and location. Owners from other cities in Cambodia can ask whether transportation can be arranged.

Questions about cat boarding

Is Pet House a cattery?

No. We provide home-based care in a normal residential environment rather than rows of boarding cages.

Can I visit before leaving my cat?

Yes. Contact us to discuss a pre-booking visit.

Will I receive updates?

Yes. We send regular photos and videos.

Do you also board dogs?

Yes, subject to availability and whether the mix of pets is suitable. Tell us about your cat's experience around dogs.
