# PET HOUSE — Pet boarding & pet sitting in Phnom Penh, Cambodia

Official website for **Pet House**, a small home-based pet boarding service in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. We care for dogs and cats in an air-conditioned home, without traditional cages or kennels. We offer individual attention, familiar routines, walks for dogs and regular photo/video updates. We welcome enquiries about other species, subject to individual arrangements and availability.
